ELECTRICAL DESIGN MECHANICAL DESIGN ISO 13485 / 14971 IEC 60601-1 510(k) PATHWAY

1. Background & Clinical Intent

Clinical Problem: Tortuous varicose veins are commonly treated via sclerotherapy, an uncomfortable foam injection suffering from high rates of partial or incomplete vein closure which can lead to deep vein thrombosis.

Engineering Goal: Reengineer the body and heating element of a thermal ablation catheter for flexibility, allowing this more effective treatment method to be used on extremely tortuous veins and improve patient outcomes.

2. Design Controls & Regulatory Framework

  • QMS & Risk Planning: Quality documentation and planning executed under ISO 9001, ISO 14971, and ISO 13485 standards.
  • Risk Management: Performed comprehensive user and design FMEA analyses.
  • Standards Integration: Researched and integrated other ISO, IEC, & IPC consensus standards for biocompatibility, catheter design, packaging, and electrical safety.
  • Regulatory Pathway: Structured documentation around a 510(k) premarket notification pathway.

3. Design

Requirements Flow: User needs → product requirements → detailed design inputs.

Structural Elements

Nitinol braid to provide mechanical structure and protect internal wiring.

Jacket Design

Hybrid durometer jacket engineered to balance flexibility and structure for steerability.

Thermal Management & Heating

Spiral-cut polyimide tubing implemented for thermal protection, paired with variable pitch nitinol heating element windings.

Finishing & Sterilization

PET heat shrink and cyanoacrylate tip applied to reduce friction, protect electronics, and enable ethylene oxide sterilization.

Ergonomic Handle

Injection-molded handle housing internal support for custom PCB and wiring, a catheter port, power cable, luer-lock compatible guidewire access, and control switch.

4. Prototype Assembly

Hand-assembled proof of concept fabricated using a catheter reflow oven, evaluated against predicate hardware using custom 3D-printed testing blocks.
